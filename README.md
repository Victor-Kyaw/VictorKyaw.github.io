# My Cybersecurity Portfolio

## Project: Internal Security Audit & Compliance Gap Analysis

* Evaluated a retail organisation's technical and physical security posture against PCI DSS and GDPR frameworks to identify vulnerabilities and assess regulatory compliance.
* Identified critical deficiencies in internal access management, noting the absence of Least Privilege architecture and separation of duties, which exposed sensitive cardholder data and PII/SPII to non-essential personnel.
* Highlighted severe gaps in data confidentiality, specifically detailing the lack of baseline encryption for financial transactions and E.U. customer data stored within internal databases.
* Assessed incident readiness and business continuity capabilities, validating existing 72-hour GDPR breach notification policies while identifying the critical absence of formal disaster recovery plans and automated data backups.
* Formulated actionable remediation strategies for corporate leadership, prioritising the deployment of strong encryption protocols, centralised password management, and the immediate implementation of an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) to improve network visibility.
